Yes. Tap **Suggest moments**, and the app looks through the video for saves and goalie action you didn't mark.
It all happens on your phone. You keep or skip each one.

<Note>
**Suggest moments** shows on newer iPhones and iPads, and on a Mac with an Apple chip.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug in first">
    Plug your phone in, if you can.

    It's a lot of work for the phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    In **Suggestions**, tap **Suggest moments**.

    Keep the app open until it's done.

    <S7_14 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch each one">
    Tap a suggestion to watch it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep or skip it">
    Tap **Keep as a save** or **Skip**.

    <S7_15 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
