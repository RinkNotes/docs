Press and hold to end each period, and tap **Start P2** at the next faceoff. After the last period, tap **Game over**.
Each period is its own part of the game, so your marks know when they happened.

## Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the warm-up, if you like">
    Tap **Start warm-up** while the teams skate.

    Then tap **Start P1** at the faceoff. There is no gap in the video.

    <S2_9 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pause and periods

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause">
    Tap **Pause** to stop for a bit.

    While it's paused, marks don't count.

    <S2_19 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go on">
    Tap **Resume P1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the period">
    Press and hold **Hold to end P1** for one second.

    It's a hold, so a stray tap can't end it.

    <S2_20 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the next period">
    Tap **Start P2** at the faceoff.

    To fix a mark first, tap **Review marks**.

    <S2_21 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The end

<Steps>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the final score">
    Tap **Yes, the game is over** when the score is right.

    Wrong score? Tap **Fix the score first**.

    <S2_22 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Left the app at a break?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick up where you stopped">
    In the Library, tap **Continue** under the game.

    The game goes on where it stopped, with its score.

    <S2_23 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
