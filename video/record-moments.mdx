Not the whole game? Tap **Moments**. Each tap of **Record** makes one short clip, up to 5 minutes long.
It's good for a practice, a skills day, or one player's shifts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Moments">
    In the Library, tap **Moments**.

    <S4_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.

    A place is up to you. Lens, holding and focus work just as for a game.

    <S4_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a moment">
    Tap **Record** when the moment starts.

    <S4_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop when it's over">
    Tap **Stop**.

    At 5 minutes it stops by itself. Tap **Record** for the next one.

    <S4_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it was">
    Tap **Save**, or another word, or **Skip**.

    The word helps build your reel. You can change it later.

    <S4_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Tap **Finish** when you're all done.

    <S4_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say yes">
    Tap **Yes, finish**.

    Not done yet? Go back to the Library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record more later">
    On the session, tap **Record more moments**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
