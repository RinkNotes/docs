On a game, tap **Ask** and type or say what you want, like "blocker saves in the second". Tap a result to watch it.
Ask can also find words said in the game. What it hears stays on your phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask">
    On the game, tap **Ask**.

    <S8_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Type what you want, like "blocker saves in the second".

    Tap a result to watch it.

    <S8_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or say it">
    Press and hold the microphone while you talk.

    It only listens while you hold it.

    <S8_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what was said">
    Ask for a word, like "where does someone say trainer".

    <S8_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it listen">
    Tap **Listen to this game** when Ask offers it.

    What it hears stays on your phone, and is never saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep what you find">
    Tap **Keep as a mark** to keep a result.

    Then tag it like any other moment.

    <S8_5 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
