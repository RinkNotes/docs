Your marks become moments. Open the game, tap a moment, and say what happened, like a save or a goal.
You can trim a moment, add one you missed, or leave one out of the reel.

## Read the lines

Each letter on the lines is a moment:

| Letter | Means |
| --- | --- |
| S | A save |
| G | A goal for |
| A | A goal against |
| O | Other |
| M | A mark with no tag yet |

A purple person on a letter is a [player moment](/video/player-moments).

## Say what happened

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    In the Library, tap the game.

    <S7_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a moment">
    Tap a moment in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what happened">
    Tap **Save**, **Goal for**, **Goal against** or **Other**.

    <S7_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the save type">
    Tap the kind of save, like **Glove**.

    Tap **Home** or **Away** to say whose save it was.

    <S7_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="All the saves at once">
    Tap **All to Home** if most saves were your goalie's.

    Then you only fix the few that weren't.

    <S7_4 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trim and add

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the start">
    Tap **Start here** where it should start.

    <S7_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the end">
    Tap **End here** where it should end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a moment you missed">
    Tap **New moment here** where you want it.

    The video stays where it is.

    <S7_6 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## In or out, fast or slow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Leave a moment out">
    Turn off **In the reel**.

    <S7_11 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replay it slowly">
    Turn on **Slow motion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soft moments">
    Read the amber note on a soft moment.

    A striped amber line shows where the picture was soft.

    <S7_12 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upside down?">
    Tap **Turn 180°** on that moment.

    The app turns most upside-down bits by itself.

    <S7_13 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Made a mistake? Tap **Undo** at the top. It's always there.
</Tip>
