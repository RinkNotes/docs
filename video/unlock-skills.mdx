Skills is for goalie coaches, and it needs a coach code from RinkNotes. Type or scan the code once, then add the places you teach.
No code? Skills stays locked, and nothing else changes.

<Note>
To get a coach code, write to [rinknotes@wispera.ai](mailto:rinknotes@wispera.ai). The goalie is shown by number only, never by name.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Skills">
    In the Library, tap **Skills**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your code">
    Type the coach code from RinkNotes.

    Or tap **Scan the QR code**, and point the camera at the code.

    <S5_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock it">
    Tap **Unlock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a place">
    On your academy's page, tap **Add a location**.

    Each place keeps its own lens, holding and focus.

    <S5_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the place">
    Type the place's name under Location name.

    A rink you used before is under Places on this iPhone.

    <S5_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
