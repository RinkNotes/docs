Tap **MARK** right after something good happens. Tap a save button after a save, and a goal button after a goal.
Your reels come from these marks. Goals keep the score for you.

## Mark as you go

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the big moments">
    Tap **MARK** right after something good happens.

    Marks are where your reel comes from.

    <S2_10 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the saves">
    Tap **Home save** or **Away save** after a save.

    Then the goalie reel uses them.

    <S2_11 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the goals">
    Tap **Goal for** when your team scores.

    Tap **Goal against** for the other team. The score goes up by itself.

    <S2_12 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark without looking">
    Press a volume button to mark.

    Camera Control, a click on an AirPods stem, and a gimbal's button mark too.

    <S2_15 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix a mistake

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tapped a goal by mistake?">
    Tap **Undo** in the note right away.

    The note stays for 5 seconds.

    <S2_13 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the score">
    Tap the score.

    Tap − or + for each team. You can do it any time, even between periods.

    <S2_14 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close it">
    Tap **Done**.

    It changes the score only. Your goal marks stay as they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things you'll see

<Steps>
  <Step title="New clips start by themselves">
    Keep going when you see a new clip start.

    A new clip starts every 5 minutes, with nothing lost.

    <S2_16 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="The screen goes dim">
    Tap the screen to make it bright again.

    It dims after 30 seconds to save battery. **MARK** still works.

    <S2_17 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
