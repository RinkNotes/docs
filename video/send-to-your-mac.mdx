sidebarTitle: "Send a game to your Mac"

---

import { S9_1, S9_2, S9_3, S9_4, S9_5 } from "/snippets/video/send.jsx";

On the game, tap **Details**, then **Send to Mac**. Send it by AirDrop, a cable, or iCloud Drive.
The Mac has more room and more tools. A game you send there opens just as it was.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game's details">
    On the game, tap **Details**.

    <S9_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Send to Mac">
    Tap **Send to Mac**.

    <S9_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it by AirDrop">
    Tap **AirDrop to a Mac…**.

    Keep this screen open until it's sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Or use a cable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug in">
    Plug the phone into the Mac.

    <S9_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Finder">
    On the Mac, open Finder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the game">
    In Finder, pick your phone.

    Pick Files, then RN Video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it over">
    Drag the game onto RN Video on the Mac.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Or use iCloud Drive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save it to iCloud Drive">
    Tap **Save to Files…**.

    Pick iCloud Drive. It's good when the Mac isn't near you.

    <S9_4 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free up space

<Steps>
  <Step title="Delete it from the phone">
    Once it's on your Mac, tap **Delete from this iPhone…**

    This can't be undone, so check the Mac has it first.

    <S9_5 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
