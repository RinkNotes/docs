import { S8_6, S8_7, S8_8 } from "/snippets/video/ask.jsx";

Tap **Look closer** under the player. Step one frame at a time, pinch to zoom in, and follow one player.
When you find the exact frame, tap **This is the moment**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look closer">
    Tap **Look closer** under the player.

    Tap the arrows to step one frame at a time. Pinch to zoom in, and tap twice to see it all.

    <S8_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a player">
    Tap a player's box to stay on them.

    It can slip to someone else, so watch it. If it slips, tap the right player.

    <S8_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the exact frame">
    Tap **This is the moment**.

    <S8_8 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back">
    Tap **Done**.

    The mark is the only thing kept from here.
  </Step>
</Steps>
