Set up once at each rink: how to hold the phone, a lens, and the focus. RN Video keeps them for that rink.
These are on the New game sheet, before you tap **Ready**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick which way to hold it">
    Tap **Landscape** to keep the whole play.

    Pick **Portrait** to follow one skater.

    <S2_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a lens">
    Tap **2x** to start.

    Use **1x** close to the ice. Use **Telephoto** high in the stands.

    <S2_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep focus locked">
    Leave Focus on **Locked on the ice**.

    Locked focus never drifts to the glass or the net.

    <S2_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aim at the ice">
    Put the phone on a tripod, aimed at centre ice.

    Check that it says Focus locked. If not, aim at the ice and tap **Refocus**.

    <S2_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the grey line">
    Look at the grey line under the picture.

    It says how long your storage and battery last.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the picture goes soft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refocus while you record">
    Press and hold **Hold to refocus** for one second.

    It's a hold, so a bump can't change the focus.

    <S2_18 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refocus between periods">
    Tap **Refocus**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
See an amber line about holding it? The phone is held the other way. Turn it to match, or tap **Change**.
</Tip>
