Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game, then **Start P1** at the faceoff. Everything works as in a game, and the score shows on the stream.
If the internet drops, the stream tries again by itself. Your recording is safe.

<Warning>
Before you go live, ask the rink if streaming is allowed. Make sure no one else has the rights to the game. Tell the families it will be streamed.
</Warning>

## Before the game

Set up where it goes first: [YouTube](/get-started/stream-to-youtube), [Facebook](/get-started/stream-to-facebook), [Vimeo](/get-started/stream-to-vimeo) or [another address](/get-started/stream-to-any-address).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the short names">
    Look at the short names under Teams.

    The score on the stream uses them, like NOR and HAR. Tap **Change** to pick your own, up to 4 letters.

    <S3_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the quality">
    Leave Stream quality on **720p**.

    Pick **1080p** only if the rink's internet is fast.

    <S3_7 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.

    **Go live** starts the stream. **Start P1** starts the recording, as in a game.

    <S3_9 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for ON AIR">
    Look for ON AIR and sending well at the top.

    **MARK**, the saves and the goals work as in a game. Goals change the score on the stream too.

    <S3_10 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="If the internet drops">
    Keep recording when you see RECONNECTING.

    It tries again by itself, and your recording is safe.

    <S3_11 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stop and end

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop the stream">
    Tap **Stop stream** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say yes">
    Tap **Stop stream** again.

    It says OFF AIR, and the recording goes on. Tap **Go live again** to start again.

    <S3_12 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tapped Home by mistake?">
    Tap **Keep streaming**.

    Going home ends the stream.

    <S3_13 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.

    Game over ends the stream too.

    <S3_14 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Calling the game

Want to talk over the game, like on TV?

- **Use a wired mic:** plug it into the streaming phone, or sit right beside it. AirPods don't work for the voice.
- **Your voice is in the recording:** when you make a reel, pick Sound **Off** or **Remove nearby voices**.
- **Send the link yourself:** RN Video doesn't send it. Share it from YouTube or Facebook.
