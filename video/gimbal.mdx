Put your iPhone on a gimbal on a tripod, and tap **Follow the play**. It turns left and right with the play, so you can watch the game.
We test with the Insta360 Flow 2 Pro.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phone in first">
    Put your phone in the gimbal before you turn it on.

    It connects by itself when it turns on.

    <S6_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the setup once">
    On the Ready screen, tap **Gimbal setup**.

    Do the three steps under Once. Then tap **Test the gimbal**.

    <S6_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the play">
    Tap **Follow the play** on the Ready screen.

    **Hold** keeps the gimbal still. It's good for a goalie game.

    <S6_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the ends of the rink">
    Tap **Set the rink's ends** on the Ready screen.

    It never turns past the ends.

    <S6_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the left end">
    Hold an arrow until the green line is at the left end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the left end">
    Tap **Left end here**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the right end">
    Hold an arrow until the line is at the right end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the right end">
    Tap **Right end here**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the game

<Steps>
  <Step title="It waits for the faceoff">
    Tap **Puck dropped** if it's still waiting after the faceoff.

    In the warm-up there's no single play to follow.

    <S6_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Help it turn">
    Press and hold an arrow to turn it yourself.

    Turning the gimbal by hand can switch it off.

    <S6_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold it still for a bit">
    Press and hold **Hold to stop following** for one second.

    The strip says HOLDING. The arrows still work.

    <S6_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow again">
    Press and hold **Hold to follow** for one second.

    It's a hold, so a bump can't change it.

    <S6_8 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Gimbal trouble

- **"Connected but not answering":** the Insta360 app may be holding it. Do step 1 in **Gimbal setup**.
- **"Gimbal disconnected":** turn it off and on, then put the phone back in. Recording goes on.
- **It stops following:** it holds still when it can't follow. It starts again by itself.
