import { S7_7, S7_8, S7_9, S7_10 } from "/snippets/video/reel.jsx";

Turn on **Player moment** for a moment, and add the jersey number. Then tap that number over the list to see only that player.
Players are shown by number only. The app never asks for a name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make it a player moment">
    On a picked moment, turn on **Player moment**.

    <S7_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the number">
    Tap **Add number**.

    <S7_8 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type it">
    Type the jersey number.

    Numbers you used before show with a +. Tap one to add it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Add**.

    A moment can have more than one player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One number for all">
    Tap the Give button to number every player moment at once.

    It's good when the moments are all your own child.

    <S7_9 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show one player">
    Tap a number over the list, like #17.

    Tap **Player moments** for all of them, or **All** to see every moment.

    <S7_10 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
