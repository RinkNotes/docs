In the Library, tap **Import videos** and pick them from Photos or Files. They become a Moments session you can tag and make reels from.
Your own videos aren't changed. RN Video works on copies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import videos">
    In the Library, tap **Import videos**.

    <S4_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where they are">
    Tap **From Photos** or **From Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your videos">
    Pick the videos you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring them in">
    Tap **Import**.

    Team names and a place are up to you. Each video of 5 minutes or less becomes one moment.

    <S4_8 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the copy">
    Keep RN Video open until it's done.

    Then the new session opens on its moments.

    <S4_9 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add more later

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add videos">
    In a Moments session, tap **Add videos**.

    They go after the last moment.

    <S4_10 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
