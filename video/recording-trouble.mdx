---
title: "What if something goes wrong while I record a hockey game?"
sidebarTitle: "When recording goes wrong"
description: "A call, a hot phone, low battery or a soft picture: RN Video keeps every clip. Here's what it says, and what to do."
---

Every clip is kept, whatever happens. RN Video tells you what went wrong, and what to tap to go on.
Here's what it says, and what to do.

## A call, or you left the app

It pauses. It starts again by itself when you come back. Open RN Video to keep going.

To stop calls, turn on **Do Not Disturb** in Control Centre before the game.

## The phone gets hot

When it's hot, it keeps going, a bit less smooth. When it's too hot, it stops.

Let it cool in the shade. Then tap **Resume P1**.

## Battery or space runs low

It warns you first. If it stops, every clip is saved.

Charge the phone or free some space. Then tap **Resume P1**.

## "RN Video needs the camera and microphone"

Open the Settings app, then RN Video. Turn on Camera and Microphone.

## The picture is soft

Press and hold **Hold to refocus**. Between periods, tap **Refocus**.

## An amber line about holding it

The phone is held the other way. Turn it to match, or tap **Change**.

## You left the app at a break

In the Library, tap **Continue** under the game. It picks up where it stopped, with its score.

<Tip>
Bring a tripod and power if you can. A game takes about 20 to 25 GB an hour.
</Tip>
