Put the phone on a tripod with the goalie and the net in view. Tap **Next drill** as each drill starts, and the recording carries straight on.
Each moment then reads by its drill, like T-push.

## Before the lesson

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the place">
    On the academy page, tap where you're teaching today.

    To take a place off the list, tap ⋯ and **Remove location**.

    <S5_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the goalie's number">
    Type the goalie's number under Goalie.

    The app never asks for a name.

    <S5_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the drills, if you like">
    Tap **Add a drill**.

    Pick a drill. Add them in the order you'll teach them.

    <S5_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="No plan?">
    Say "Mark" and the drill's name as each drill starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the lesson">
    Tap **Start recording**.

    Aim at the goalie and the net, as for a game.

    <S5_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go drill by drill">
    Tap **Next drill** when the next drill starts.

    Tap **MARK** after a good rep. The volume buttons and AirPods mark too.

    <S5_8 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the session">
    Press and hold **Hold to end session** for one second.

    Need a break part-way? Tap **Pause**.

    <S5_9 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the lesson

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the session">
    In the Library, tap the session.

    Each moment shows its drill's name and time. You tag and trim them as for a game.

    <S5_10 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to the Mac">
    On the session, tap **Details**.

    Then tap **Send to Mac**. On the Mac, it opens just like a game.

    <S5_11 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Send a game to your Mac](/video/send-to-your-mac).
