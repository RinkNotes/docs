RN Video opens on your Library. The four ways to record are at the top: **Game**, **Stream**, **Moments** and **Skills**.
Your games and sessions show under them.

## The four ways to record

| Pick | To |
| --- | --- |
| **Game** | Record a whole game, period by period. |
| **Stream** | Send a game live, and record it too. |
| **Moments** | Record short clips, up to 5 minutes each. |
| **Skills** | Record a goalie's lesson, drill by drill. It needs a coach code. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick how to record">
    Tap **Game** to record a whole game.

    <S1_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the short notice">
    Tap **Start recording**.

    The notice shows only the first time.

    <S1_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Videos you already have">
    Tap **Import videos**.

    Pick **From Photos** or **From Files**. They become a Moments session.

    <S1_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it learned hockey">
    At the end of the Library, tap **Acknowledgements**.

    It says how the app learned to see hockey.

    <S1_4 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
