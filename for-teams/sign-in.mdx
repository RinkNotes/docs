Open the invite we email you, on the iPhone, iPad or Mac you'll use. Sign in with Apple, Google or an email link.
You only need an account to publish or stream to a team page. Watching never needs one.

## Join with your invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite">
    Open the invite email on the device with RN Video.

    An invite works for 14 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how to sign in">
    Tap **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google** or **Email me a link**.

    There's no password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    If you picked the email, open the link we send on this device.

    Or type the code from the same email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Tap **Done**.

    It shows your team, and what you can do on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You must be 18 or older to join. The link and the code in our email work for 15 minutes. Tap **Send it again** for a new one.
</Note>

## Join with a code

Got an invite code instead?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    In the Library, tap **Team**, near the end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    Type the code under **Invite code**.

    Or tap **Scan the QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join">
    Tap **Join**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
