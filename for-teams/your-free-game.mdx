Yes. A new team page starts as a free trial, with one game on it.
You have 5 days after you first publish to upgrade.

## What the free game includes

- The game's highlights.
- The game's goalie reel.
- Up to 3 player reels. Coming.
- Up to 2 coaches, who can make up to 3 feedback videos. Coming.

A trial doesn't include the full game, or going live on the team page.
You can still go live free on your own YouTube or Facebook.

## How the trial works

- Publish the reels from one game. Your families watch them on the team page.
- The trial ends 5 days after the first reel is on the page.
- We email you on day 3, to remind you.
- The team page shows the day the trial ends.
- To publish a second game, your team needs to upgrade.

## When the trial ends

If your team hasn't upgraded by day 5:

- you can't publish any more;
- the team page stops playing, and says the trial has ended;
- we email you that day, and again on day 8.

On day 10, we delete everything on the page.
Upgrade before then, and it all comes back.

## If you never publish

We remove the team 14 days after we made it.
We email you on day 10, to remind you.

## What we delete at the end

If your team doesn't upgrade, we delete:

- the team page;
- its reels;
- its invites, and everyone's place on the team.

Then we send one last email to say it's done.

<Note>
The video on your own iPhone, iPad or Mac stays. We never delete that.
</Note>

## How to upgrade

1. We send you a payment link by email. You can ask us for it at any time.
2. Pay with the link.
3. Once we see the payment, we mark your team as paid.

Your team page and its reels stay, and the end date goes away.

A team page is from C$249 a season. That's our starting price, for the number of games most teams play.
It depends on your team's age, level and games.
Planned prices, in Canadian dollars, plus tax. See [what it costs](https://rinknotes.app/pricing/) to work out your team's price.

Going live on the team page comes with a team plan.
