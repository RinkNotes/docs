Send the short form at [rinknotes.app/team-page/request](https://rinknotes.app/team-page/request/). It takes a minute.
We email you a copy. Then we set up your team page and send you an invite.

## What the form asks

- Your team's name.
- Its age group, like U11 or U15.
- Its level, like house, A, AA or AAA. You can skip this.
- Your name.
- Your email.

## What happens next

1. The page says "Thanks, we've got it."
2. We email you a copy of your request.
3. We set up your team page. We write to you if we have a question.
4. You get an invite by email. Open it, and join your team in RN Video.
5. Your team gets one free game on its page. See [your free game](/for-teams/your-free-game).

You can publish reels to the page as soon as you join.

## What we keep

We keep your request for 24 months.
It holds your team's name, age group and level, and your name and email.

Questions? Write to us at [rinknotes@wispera.ai](mailto:rinknotes@wispera.ai).

## What it costs

Asking is free, and so is your first game.
After that, a team page is from C$249 a season. It depends on your team's age, level and games.

Planned prices, in Canadian dollars, plus tax. See [what it costs](https://rinknotes.app/pricing/) to work out your team's price.
