title: "How do I put a reel on my team's page?"

---

Make the reel, then tap **Publish…** beside **Save to Photos**. Pick the game, and tap **Publish**.
Families watch it on the team page from a link. They need no app and no account.

<Note>
**Publish…** shows once you've joined a team as a publisher. See [sign in and join your team](/for-teams/sign-in).
</Note>

## Publish a game's reels

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the reel">
    Make a game reel or a goalie reel.

    See [make highlights and a goalie reel](/get-started/highlights-and-goalie-reel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Publish">
    When the reel is done, tap **Publish…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the game">
    Tap **Game**.

    Pick the game on the team page.

    No game yet? Tap **A new game**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wi-Fi or not">
    Leave **Upload on cellular** off to wait for Wi-Fi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Tap **Publish**.

    You can keep working while it uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When it's done, it says **On the team page**. Tap **Copy link** to send it to the families.

## Publish your moments

In a Moments session, tap **Publish…**, and pick the game.

## On a Mac

Click **Export**. When it's done, click **Publish…** beside **Show in Finder**.

## Good to know

- Someone on your team may have started the game already. Pick it, so it's all on one page.
- Publish the same reel again, and the app asks to **Replace it**.
- A full game is kept 60 days. Reels stay until the team takes them down.
- To take a reel down, ask your team. It stops playing within a minute.
