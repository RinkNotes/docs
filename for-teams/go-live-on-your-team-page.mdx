With a team plan, tap **Stream**, then pick **Our team page (live)** under **Where it goes**. Tap **Go live**, and send the watch link.
There's nothing to set up. The stream and its watch link are ready right away, so you can go live today.

<Note>
Going live on the team page needs a team plan. A free trial can't. Anyone can stream free to [YouTube](/get-started/stream-to-youtube), [Facebook](/get-started/stream-to-facebook) or [any stream address](/get-started/stream-to-any-address).
</Note>

## Two ways to go live

| | Free, on YouTube or Facebook | On your team page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | C$7 an hour in a season block, or C$9 an hour game by game |
| Who watches | Anyone with the YouTube or Facebook link | Anyone with your team's link |
| Sign-in | Some viewers may need to sign in | No app, no account, no sign-in |
| Around the game | Their comments, suggested videos and ads | Only your team's games |
| The replay | You publish it from the phone after the game | On your team page right away |
| Setting it up | Your channel's stream key. YouTube can take a day. | Nothing. Ready right away. |

Planned prices, in Canadian dollars, plus tax.

## Go live

We set up your team's streaming phone with you. Then **Our team page (live)** shows under Other ways.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    In the Library, tap **Stream**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it goes">
    Tap **Where it goes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the team page">
    Under Other ways, tap **Our team page (live)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.

    Look for ON AIR at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Tap **Send watch link**.

    Families watch with just the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the period">
    Tap **Start P1** at the faceoff.

    This starts the recording. **MARK** and the goals work as in a game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.

    The game becomes the replay on your team page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Before you go live, ask the rink if streaming is allowed. Tell the families the game will be streamed.
</Warning>

See [what it costs](https://rinknotes.app/pricing/) to work out your team's live hours.
