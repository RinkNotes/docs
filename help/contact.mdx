Email us at [hello@wispera.ai](mailto:hello@wispera.ai). If you test an app in TestFlight, you can send feedback from there too.
Anything that's hard, slow or wrong helps us. So does anything you liked.

## From TestFlight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take a screenshot">
    Press the side button and volume up together.

    Do it in the app, on the screen you want to tell us about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the screenshot">
    Tap the small picture in the corner.

    You can draw on it if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with us">
    Tap **Share**.

    Pick **Share Beta Feedback**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Write a note.

    Tap **Submit** to send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

No screenshot? Open **TestFlight**, tap the app, and then tap **Send Beta Feedback**.

Joined with the public link? Your feedback reaches us without your name. If you'd like a reply, add your email to your note.

<Warning>
Protect your players. A screenshot of your roster, your card or your notes shows real kids' names. Cover the names with the pen first, or tell us in words.
</Warning>

## What helps most

- What you did, what you thought would happen, and what did.
- Your phone, and which way you held it.
- For a recording: marks that were missed, or came out late.
- For a stream: where it went, and if it dropped.
- For a gimbal: when it lost the play.
- For a roster from screenshots: how many players it found, and how many you fixed.
- For AirPods notes: which AirPods, if the phone was locked, and any press it missed.
- For Siri: which phrase you said, and what Siri did.
- For sending a team: how you sent it, and if it opened.
- Which words in the app made you stop and think.
