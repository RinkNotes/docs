sidebarTitle: "Good to know"

---

Both apps work at the rink with no signal, no Wi-Fi and no account. Your video and your notes stay with you.
They leave only when you stream, send or share them.

## RN Video

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your video stays with you" icon="lock">
    The app sends nothing to us. Video leaves only when you stream it, send it to your Mac, or share it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Records at the rink" icon="wifi-slash">
    To record, you need no signal, no Wi-Fi and no account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Streaming needs the internet" icon="tower-broadcast">
    Use good Wi-Fi or a strong phone signal. If it drops, the recording carries on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A game takes a lot of space" icon="film">
    About 20 to 25 GB an hour. The Library says how much time is left.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring power" icon="battery-half">
    A whole game can use most of a battery. A power bank helps, most of all with a gimbal or a stream.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It sees players, not people" icon="magnifying-glass">
    It finds goalies, skaters, referees and nets. It never knows who anyone is. It never reads numbers. Numbers come only from you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You can always take it back" icon="rotate-left">
    Tap **Undo** at the top of a game to take back a change.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Lineups

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Works at the rink" icon="wifi-slash">
    No signal, no Wi-Fi and no account needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Screenshots aren't kept" icon="eye-slash">
    They're read on your phone. Only names, numbers and positions are saved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notes stay private" icon="lock">
    A shared card never has your notes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Picks up where you left off" icon="rotate-right">
    Close the app mid-setup, and it opens on the same step.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dark at first" icon="moon">
    It's easier to read under arena lights. Pick Light in **Appearance**, in Settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Separate teams" icon="users">
    Each team has its own roster, games and notes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Topics stay on your phone" icon="lock">
    They're worked out each time you look, and never saved.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
