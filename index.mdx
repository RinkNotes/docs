RinkNotes makes two apps for hockey. Video films your games, streams them, and makes the highlights.
Lineups puts your lineup card on your phone, and writes down what you say on the bench.

## Video by RinkNotes

Your games, filmed and shared.

- Film the whole game in sharp 4K, or just the big moments.
- Stream it live to YouTube or Facebook.
- Get highlights and a goalie reel, made for you.
- Watch any play slowly, and zoom in.

It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The iPhone and iPad film. The Mac is where you tag games and make reels.

## Lineups

Your lineup card, on your phone.

- Add your team from a screenshot.
- Drag players into lines.
- Talk during the game, and it writes your notes.
- Send parents a short update after.

It works on iPhone.

## Video, voice and AI

Your phone is the camera, the microphone and the brains. You pick what you need for each game.

- **Video:** it films the game, with the rink's sound. On a gimbal, it follows the play.
- **Voice:** it hears you. Talk on the bench, and Lineups writes your notes.
- **AI:** it finds the saves, goals and big plays, and makes the reels.

The hockey AI runs right on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, with no internet. It sees "a goalie" or "a skater", never which one.
It doesn't look at faces or read jersey numbers.

## Your video stays with you

What you record stays on your devices. It leaves only when you stream it, send it to your Mac, or share it.
Lineups keeps your notes on your phone. Your notes are never on a card you share.

## Both apps are in beta

Both apps are free to try now, through Apple's TestFlight app.
