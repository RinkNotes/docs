Recording, highlights, goalie reels, streaming to YouTube or Facebook, and all of Lineups are free. You don't need an account.
A team plan adds one page for your team's games. We set it up for you.

## Free, for anyone

You don't need an account for any of this.

- Record games, moments and practices.
- Get highlights and goalie reels, with slow motion.
- Look closer at any play, and ask for what you want to see.
- Save a reel as a video file, and post it anywhere.
- Stream to YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo or any stream address.
- Lineups: lines, voice notes and parent updates.

<Note>
Finding the saves you missed works on a Mac, or on newer iPhones and iPads.
Vimeo needs a Vimeo plan that includes live.
</Note>

## Team plan: we set it up for your team

A team plan is from C$249 a season. It depends on your team's age, level and games.

- **Team page:** every game, its highlights, goalie reel and full game, on one page families open from a link.
- **Publishing:** the parents who film get an invite, and their reels go straight to the page.
- **Live on the team page:** in HD, watched with just a link. C$7 an hour in a season block, or C$9 an hour game by game.
- **Stats:** saves for each goalie. Shots, save percentage and more are Coming.
- **Coach marks:** coaches mark moments on the team page. Coming.

Planned prices, in Canadian dollars, plus tax. See [what it costs](https://rinknotes.app/pricing/) to work out your team's price.

## How to get a team plan

Send the short form to [request a team page](/for-teams/request-a-team-page). Your team starts with [one free game](/for-teams/your-free-game).

We make the team. Then we send each person an invite by email. An invite can make someone a publisher, a streamer or a coach.

- A publisher puts reels on the team page.
- A streamer goes live on the team page.
- A coach will mark moments and add notes. Coming.
