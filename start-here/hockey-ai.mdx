The hockey AI helps you find the plays, follows the play on a gimbal, and writes down your notes.
Runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, with no internet. You decide what to keep.

## Small AI that does big AI jobs

A small AI made just for hockey. Does jobs people pay big AI for, like helping you find the plays and writing down your notes.
Nothing to pay each time you use it.

## Runs on your device

Looks at your video right on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. No internet needed.
Your video is never sent away to be looked at.

## What it does today

- **Follows the play:** on a gimbal, turns your phone to follow the play by itself.
- **Helps you find the plays:** suggests saves, goals and big plays to tag. You keep or skip each one.
- **Answers you:** ask for what you want to see, like "every glove save in the third".
- **Makes the reels:** frames each replay on the goalie, with slow motion.
- **Reads your team list:** Lineups adds your team from a screenshot.
- **Writes your notes:** say "Start listening in Lineups", and it writes the note.

Everything it does in the apps today runs on your device, with no large language model.

## What it doesn't do

- Today, sees "a goalie" or "a skater", never which one.
- Doesn't look at faces.
- Doesn't read jersey numbers. Numbers come only from you.
