On a player's notes, tap **Summary**. Lineups drafts what they did well and what to work on, from your notes.
You check it, and share only what you approve.

## Make a summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    On a player's notes, tap **Summary**.

    On a team's **Notes** tab, each player's group has **Summary** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the time">
    Tap **Game**, **Week**, **Month**, **Mid-season** or **End of season**.

    **Earlier** and **Later** step back and forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the draft">
    Read the sentences under each point.

    They come from your notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix a sentence">
    Tap a sentence that's on the wrong side, or in the wrong area.

    Lineups remembers your fixes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the points you agree with">
    Tap **Accept** on each point you want, or **Drop**.

    A goalie's points are in already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own words">
    Tap **Add a line** under any point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve it">
    Tap **Approve**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Tap **Share as text** or **Share as image**.

    Only what you approved is shared.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Your notes' own words go in only if you turn on **Quote this note**.
- Turn on **Game stats** to add goals, assists and +/−. Keep or drop each line.
- Not many notes yet? The draft says so. Write your own lines instead.
- Mid-season has a **Through** date you can change.
