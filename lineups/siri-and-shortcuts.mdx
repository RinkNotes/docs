---
title: "Can I run Lineups with Siri while I'm on the bench?"
sidebarTitle: "Siri and shortcuts"
description: "Say a phrase like Open my game in Lineups. Or put a Lineups shortcut on the Action Button."
---

Say a phrase like "Open my game in Lineups". Siri opens your live game, or the next game's card.
At the rink, hold the side button while you say it.

## The phrases

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Open my game in Lineups" | Your live game, or the next game's card |
| "Show my lines in Lineups" | The live game's lines |
| "Listen for a player in Lineups" or "Start listening in Lineups" | A voice note |
| "Start the next period in Lineups" | The next period. Siri says which one it is. |
| "Share my lines in Lineups" | The card, ready to send |
| "Players to sort in Lineups" | Notes waiting under Needs a player |

You can also say "Go to the next period in Lineups".

## A voice note by Siri

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say the phrase">
    Say "Listen for a player in Lineups".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Listening">
    Wait until Lineups shows **Listening**.

    Words you say before that go to Siri, not Lineups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say the number and the note">
    Say "Seventeen, nice backcheck."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
With AirPods notes on, "Hey Siri" won't hear you. Hold the side button instead.
</Tip>

## Put it on the Action Button

On iPhone 15 Pro and later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the setting">
    In Settings, open Action Button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Shortcut">
    Swipe to **Shortcut**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose one">
    Tap **Choose a Shortcut**.

    Pick one under Lineups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put it on Back Tap

Back Tap lists only shortcuts saved in the Shortcuts app. Save one there first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a shortcut">
    In the Shortcuts app, tap **+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Lineups action">
    Search for Lineups, and add an action like **Open my game**.

    Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Back Tap">
    Open Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your tap">
    Choose **Double Tap** or **Triple Tap**.

    Pick your shortcut under Shortcuts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- The shortcuts don't run on a locked phone. Unlock it when Siri asks.
- Lineups lists every shortcut in Settings, under **Siri & shortcuts**.
