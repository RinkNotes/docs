Turn on AirPods notes in game mode, and lock your phone. Press an AirPod stem, and say the number, then the note.
"Seventeen, nice backcheck" is filed under #17, with the period.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    Open the live game in Lineups.

    The phone must be unlocked.

    <Game_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap AirPods">
    Tap the **AirPods** button at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the sheet">
    Read the sheet that opens.

    <Game_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Tap **Turn on**.

    The button turns green, and the orange dot shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Take a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pocket the phone">
    Lock the phone, and put it in your pocket.

    Keep your AirPods in.

    <Game_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press and talk">
    Press an AirPod stem.

    Say the number first, then the note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Stop talking, or press again.

    A note ends after 2.5 seconds of quiet, or at 60 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the notes with no number">
    Open **Needs a player**.

    It's in game mode, and on the team's **Notes** tab.

    <Game_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the player">
    Tap a note to choose who it's about.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before your first game

- Tap **Allow** for the microphone the first time you speak a note.
- Try one voice note while you're online. Your phone may need a one-time download.
- Test it at practice. Turn it on, lock the phone, and press and talk.
- Charge your phone.

## Watch out for

- Turn it on before you lock the phone. It can't start from a locked phone.
- It's off each time game mode opens. Turn it on for every game.
- Stay in the game. Leaving game mode, or ending the game, turns it off.
- A call or Siri turns it off. Unlock the phone, and tap **AirPods** again.
- Music pauses while it's on. The lock screen shows Lineups in Now Playing.
- The orange dot means the microphone is on. Nothing is kept until you press.
- Say the number clearly. A misheard number sends the note to Needs a player.
- Two players with the same number? The note waits under Needs a player.
- **Battery:** about 2 hours 45 minutes, locked, used about 5 % in our test.

Lineups turns notes into text on your phone. The audio is never kept.
