Tap a player, then tap where they should go. Or press and hold, and drag.
Both do the same thing. There's no Save button: every change is kept.

## Tap, then tap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick up a player">
    Tap a player on the card.

    They turn green.

    <Lines_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put them down">
    Tap the spot where they should go.

    <Lines_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Look at the card.

    On a filled spot, the two players swap.

    <Lines_3 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Tap an empty spot to pick from a list. Players who usually play there come first.
</Tip>

## Drag and drop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold a player">
    Press and hold a player or a bench chip.

    <Lines_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag over a spot">
    Drag the player over a spot.

    The spot lights up to show what will happen.

    <Lines_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let go">
    Let go over the spot.

    Drop on the tray to send them to the bench.

    <Lines_6 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

What the colours mean:

- A green outline puts the player there.
- Green **Swap** trades two players.
- Amber **Bench** means the player already there goes to the bench.

## How the card works

- One spot on the lines at a time. Moving a player to Line 3 takes them off Line 1.
- Special teams are extra. Your Line 1 centre can also play PP1 and PK1.
- Scratching takes a player off every spot. Put them on a spot to bring them back.
- Hints, not rules. A right shot on the left side is fine.

## Faster ways to fill it

- **Fill by position** makes a first draft in one tap. Goalies go in net, defence in pairs, and forwards in lines.
- **Copy last game** starts from this team's last card. Players out tonight are left off.
- On a card that already has lines, both are under **Start from**.

## Out tonight

On the team's **Roster** tab, switch a player to Out for the next game.

The card shows an amber **OUT** tag. Nothing moves until you move them.

## Undo and versions

- After a scratch, **Fill by position** or **Copy last game**, an **Undo** bar shows.
- **Versions** lists earlier cards. You can **Restore** one, or **Keep** it.

## The pre-game talk

Type one line at the bottom of the card, like "Win the wall battles."

It goes on the card image when you share it.
