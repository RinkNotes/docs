Turn your phone sideways to see the whole card, with your roster beside it. Hold it upright for quick taps.
Every screen works both ways. Nothing you do in one is lost when you turn.

## If the card won't turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Control Centre">
    Swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off the lock">
    Tap the lock with a round arrow, if it's lit up.

    It's called **Portrait Orientation Lock**. It keeps every app upright.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the phone">
    Turn it either way.

    Lineups turns with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upright: one column and a bench tray

<Turn_1 />

1. **Roster** opens the bench tray, with the whole team. Tap **Hide** to close it.
2. Bench chips: tap a chip, then a spot. Or drag the chip onto the card.
3. Hold a player near the top or bottom edge, and the card scrolls.
4. **Start game ›** is in the tray, near your thumb.

Best for quick changes at the rink door, with one hand.

## Sideways: your roster beside the whole card

<Turn_2 />

1. The roster stays in view, with where each player plays: a spot, **Bench** or **Out tonight**.
2. Forwards and defence sit side by side. Scroll down for the power play, penalty kill and Scratches.
3. To scratch a player, tap them in the roster. Then tap **Scratches**.
4. The pre-game talk and **Start game ›** sit at the bottom.

Best for building a card from scratch, and for filling the power play and penalty kill.

<Tip>
To send someone to the bench, tap them. Then tap the roster's header.
</Tip>
