Open the game's card, tap an empty spot, and tap **+ Associate player**. Pick someone, or say a new player's number, name and position.
They play that game only, with a small AP beside their name.

## Call up a player

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap an empty spot">
    On the game's card, tap the empty spot where they'll play.

    <Associates_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an associate player">
    At the bottom of the list, tap **+ Associate player**.

    <Associates_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where they come from">
    Tap a player under **Used before**, or from another team you coach.

    That adds them in one tap. For someone new, go on to the next step.

    <Associates_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Someone new? Say it">
    Under New player, tap **Say**.

    Say the number, name and position in one go. Like "Twenty-two, Sam Lee, right wing."

    <Associates_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    Fix anything Lineups misheard.

    Rather type? Tap **Type**.

    <Associates_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Tap **Add to this game**.

    They're in the spot you tapped, with a small AP.

    <Associates_6 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **+ Associate player** is also at the end of the bench, and on the team's Roster tab.
- From another team: their name, number, position, usual spot and shot are copied over.
- For that game only. They're not on your other games unless you add them.
- Notes work as for anyone, by tap, AirPods or Siri.
- **The Roster tab** lists them under **Associate players**.
- Tap one there to **Add to the roster for good**, or to **Remove** them.

## Say a whole roster

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Say it">
    On Add your roster, tap **Say it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say each player">
    Say each player's number, name and position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check every name">
    Read each name on the list.

    Speech can mishear names.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Say and Say it show on iPhones that turn speech into text on the phone. The audio is never kept.
