---
title: "How do I share my lines with another coach?"
sidebarTitle: "Share your lines"
description: "Tap share on the card. Send a card image anyone can read, or a file that opens in another coach's Lineups."
---

On the card, tap the share icon. Pick **Card image** for a picture any coach can read, or **Roster + lineup file** for an assistant's Lineups.
Your notes are never included.

## A card image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share lines">
    Tap the share icon on the card.

    It's on the Next game card too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the image">
    Tap **Card image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Share…**.

    Pick where to send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The image needs no app to read.

## A file for another coach

Send a game's roster and lines to an assistant's Lineups. Then you both work from the same card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the file">
    In Share lines, tap **Roster + lineup file**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Share…**.

    Send it by AirDrop or Mail. Messages shows the file, but can't open it in Lineups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open a file you were sent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the file">
    On your iPhone, tap the file.

    In Mail, press and hold the file first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Tap the share button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it in Lineups">
    Pick **Lineups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or update">
    Tap **Add** for a team you don't have yet.

    For a team from an earlier file, tap **Update**. It lists every change first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nobody is ever removed from your roster.

## A team you made yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update your own team">
    Tap the button with your team's name, like **Update my Wolves U13 A**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the matches">
    Check the players Lineups matched by name.

    Pair anyone it missed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Tap **Update**.

    After that, files between you match by themselves.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the file

- The roster: name, number, position, usual spot and shot.
- The game's lines and the pre-game talk.

Never your notes, goals, scorers, the final score, ratings or who's out tonight.

<Tip>
Both phones need Lineups 1.1.1 or later. A file with an associate player needs Lineups 1.3 or later.
</Tip>
