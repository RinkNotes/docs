Home lists your teams, each with what it needs next. Tap a team for its **Games**, **Roster** and **Notes** tabs.
Settings is the gear on Home.

## Home

<After_1 />

Each team shows a pill: **Add roster**, **Set lines**, **Lines set** or **Live**.

**+ Add a team** is on Home.

## A team

<After_2 />

- Games: the live game, the next game, upcoming games, past games and **Line totals**. **New game** adds one.
- Roster: out tonight, **+ Add players** from more screenshots, and each player's details.
- Notes: every note from every game.
- Switch teams from the team name at the top.
- **Team options** holds **Delete team**.

## Settings

Tap the gear on Home for your name, **Appearance** and **Siri & shortcuts**.

Lineups is dark at first. It's easier to read under arena lights. Pick Light in Appearance.

## Good to know

- Works at the rink. No signal, no Wi-Fi and no account needed.
- Screenshots aren't kept. Only names, numbers and positions are saved.
- Notes stay private. A shared card never has your notes.
- Picks up where you left off. Close the app mid-setup, and it opens on the same step.
- Separate teams. Each team has its own roster, games and notes.
