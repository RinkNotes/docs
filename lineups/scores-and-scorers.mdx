---
title: "How do I keep the score and who scored in Lineups?"
sidebarTitle: "Scores and scorers"
description: "Pick the scorer when you mark a goal for. After the game, tap Final score on the card."
---

When you mark a goal for, pick who scored and who assisted. After the game, tap **Final score** on the card.
Each player's goals, assists and +/− add up on Line totals.

## Set your lines early

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the next game's card">
    On the Games tab, tap **Edit lines**.

    On Home, **Lines** under the team opens it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it all you like">
    Move players as often as you want.

    The game goes live only when you tap **Start game**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who scored

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the goal">
    In game mode, tap **Goal for**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who was out">
    Tap the units that were on the ice.

    Players on those units come first in the lists below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the scorer">
    Tap a player under **Scored by**.

    It's optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the assists">
    Tap up to two players under the assists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Save goal**.

    **Undo** takes the whole goal back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The final score

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the finished card">
    On the Games tab, tap the game under Past.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the score">
    Tap **Final score**.

    It starts from the goals you marked. Change it to the real score.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card shows it like W 4–2, L 1–3 or T 2–2.

## Goals, assists and +/−

They show on **Line totals**, and on each player's notes.

+/− counts only the goals you marked. A power-play goal for doesn't count, nor a goal against on the penalty kill.

## Never on what you share

Scores and scorers are never on the card image, or in a roster file. In a player summary, they're in only if you turn on **Game stats**.
