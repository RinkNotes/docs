Start the game from its card, or from **Start game** on the Games tab. Then tap a player's name and speak a note.
You can also rate each line, and mark goals for and against.

<Turn_3 />

1. **Notes** and **Lines** switch between taking notes and changing the card.
2. Tap the period as the game goes on: P1, P2, P3 or OT.
3. Tap a line's label, like L1, to rate it.
4. Tap a name to take a note. The small green number counts that player's notes.
5. **Goal for** and **Goal against** are at the bottom. **End game** is under them.

## Sideways: every unit at once

<Turn_4 />

1. The team, the period and **Notes** and **Lines** fit on one row at the top.
2. All four forward lines are on the left, each with its rating button.
3. Pairs and special teams are on the right. Rate the power play the moment it ends.
4. The goal buttons and **End game** share one row at the bottom.

## Take a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the period">
    Tap the period at the top.

    Notes, ratings and goals are filed under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a name">
    Tap a player's name.

    The first time, tap **Allow** for the microphone.

    <Game_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak">
    Say your note.

    Lineups stops when you pause. It files the note under that player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or type it">
    Tap **Type instead**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Spoken notes become text on your phone. The audio isn't kept.

## Rate a line

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the label">
    Tap a unit's label, like L1, P2 or PP1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate it">
    Tap **Good**, **Okay** or **Poor**.

    The label shows the latest rating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the goal sheet">
    Tap **Goal for** or **Goal against**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who was out">
    Tap each unit that was on the ice: one line, one pair, and so on.

    <Game_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Who scored">
    On a goal for, pick the scorer under **Scored by**.

    Add up to two assists if you know them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Save goal**.

    A mistake? Tap **Undo** on the bar that shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the lines mid-game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    Tap **Lines**.

    Turn the phone sideways to see the roster beside the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to notes">
    Tap **Notes**.

    Each change is saved as a new version. The card you started with is never lost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## End the game

<Steps>
  <Step title="End it">
    Tap **End game** at the bottom.

    The card and its notes are kept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    On the Games tab, tap the game under Past.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the score">
    Tap **Final score**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ended it too soon? Tap **Reopen game** on its card.

## Good to know

- Overtime goes on past OT: OT2, OT3, and so on.
- One live game at a time. Starting a second game asks you to end the first.
- Close the app mid-game? It opens straight back into the game.
- After the game, tap a player on the finished card to add a post-game note.
- A player's notes: on the Roster tab, tap the player. Then tap **Notes**.
