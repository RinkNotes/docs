---
title: "What are the topics on my Lineups notes?"
sidebarTitle: "Topics on your notes"
description: "Each note shows up to two topics, like Skating or Compete, worked out from its own words on your phone."
---

Each note shows up to two topics, like **Skating**, **Compete** or **Hockey IQ**. They come from the note's own words.
Topics are a guide, not a verdict. Many notes have none, and that's fine.

## Filter by topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notes">
    On a team, tap the **Notes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a topic">
    Tap a topic in the row above the notes.

    Tap **All** to see every note, or **No topic** for the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A player's topics

A player's notes show their **Topics**, counted. They show once there are enough notes with a topic.

## Good to know

- Topics are worked out on your phone each time you look. They're never saved.
- They're never on a card you share.
- They work on English notes for now.
