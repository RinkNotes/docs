Click **Game** at the top. Press `↓` for the next moment, then a letter for what happened.
Keys are the fastest way to tag. The line under the timeline says what each letter means.

## Say what happened

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the next moment">
    Press `↓`.

    Press `↑` to go back. Press `space` to play it again.

    <M4_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what happened">
    Press `S` for a save.

    `G` is a goal for. `A` is a goal against.

    <M4_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the save type">
    Press a number from `1` to `8`.

    The panel shows which number is which, like 1 for Glove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Something else?">
    Click **Other** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say whose saves">
    Click **All to Home** or **All to Away**.

    Then fix the few that were the other goalie's.

    <M4_4 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## All the keys

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `↓` `↑` | Next or last moment |
| `space` | Play it again |
| `S` `G` `A` | Save, goal for, goal against |
| `1` to `8` | The save type |
| `H` | Make a save the home team's |
| `X` | Take a moment in or out of the reel |
| `R` | Slow motion |
| `I` `O` | Set the start and the end |
| `N` | Add a moment |
| `P` | Make it a player moment |
| `L` | [Look closer](/mac/ask-and-look-closer#look-closer) |

## One player's moments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a player moment">
    Press `P`.

    <M4_5 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the jersey number">
    Type the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Press `return`.

    The letter gets a purple person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show one player">
    Click a number over the timeline, like #17.

    Other moments fade. Click **All** to see them all again.

    <M4_6 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Soft or upside down

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it the right way up">
    Click **Turn 180°** on a moment filmed upside down.

    <M4_7 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave a soft one out">
    Press `X` on a moment with an amber note.

    The amber note means the picture was soft.
  </Step>
</Steps>
