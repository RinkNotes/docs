Click **Suggest moments**. It looks through the video for saves and goalie action you didn't mark.
You keep or skip each one. Nothing leaves your Mac.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Suggest moments**.

    You can keep tagging while it runs.

    <M5_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch each one">
    Watch the suggested moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it or skip it">
    Press `K` to keep it, or `⌫` to skip it.

    <M5_2 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
