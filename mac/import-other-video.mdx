Click **Import…** at the top of the window. Pick a whole game, or short clips.
Your files aren't changed. RN Video only reads them.

## Bring it in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the video">
    Click **Import…** at the top of the window.

    Choose **From Finder…** or **From Photos…**. You can also drop videos on the window.

    <M3_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Game or moments?">
    Click **Game** for a whole game, or **Moments** for short clips.

    Names are up to you.

    <M3_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring it in">
    Click **Import**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A game in clips

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say where each period starts">
    On the first clip of P2, click **P2 starts here (2)**.

    Watch the goalies. They change ends each period.

    <M3_3 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A game in one long file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the breaks">
    Click **Suggest the breaks**.

    <M3_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use them">
    Click **Use the suggested periods**.

    Check each start in the player. It can be a few seconds out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save them">
    Click **Save the periods**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add more moments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add videos">
    In a Moments session, click **Add videos…**.

    New videos go after the last moment.

    <M3_5 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
