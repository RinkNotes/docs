[Send the game from your iPhone](/video/send-to-your-mac) first. Then drag it onto the RN Video window on your Mac.
The Mac checks every file, so you know nothing was lost.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop the game in">
    Drag the game onto the RN Video window.

    Or click **Add a game…** and pick it.

    Sent it by AirDrop? Look in **Downloads**.

    <M2_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the check">
    Read the lines under the game's name.

    A tick means all is well. Amber lines say what to do next.

    <M2_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check an older game">
    Click **Check it** if a game hasn't been checked.

    **Show in Finder** shows where the game is kept.

    <M2_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a coach's lesson">
    Add a Skills session the same way as a game.

    It shows the goalie by number. Each drill gets its own line on the timeline.

    <M2_4 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
