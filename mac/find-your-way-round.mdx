Your games are in the sidebar on the left. The three steps sit at the top: **Game**, **Reel** and **Export**.
The Mac doesn't record. Record on your iPhone or iPad, then [send the game to your Mac](/video/send-to-your-mac).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a game">
    Click a game in the sidebar.

    The foot of the sidebar says how many games you have. It also says how much space they take.

    <M1_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the three steps">
    Click **Game** at the top to tag moments.

    **Reel** picks the moments and settings. **Export** makes the video.

    <M1_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the acknowledgements">
    In the **RN Video** menu, choose **Acknowledgements**.

    It lists the credits and licences.

    <M1_3 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
