Click **Export** at the top, pick a shape, and click **Export game reel…**.
You can also make a clip for every moment, or the whole game as one video.

## The reel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Export">
    Click **Export** at the top.

    <M8_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a shape">
    Pick **16:9** for a TV or laptop, or **9:16** for phones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the video">
    Click **Export game reel…**.

    The first time, pick a folder and click **Choose**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the file">
    When it's done, click **Show in Finder**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Every moment as its own clip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the clips">
    Click **Export the highlight pack…**.

    Families can pick the moments they want.

    <M8_2 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The whole game in one video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a smaller copy">
    Click **1080p copy…**.

    It's a smaller file that's easy to share.

    <M8_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or keep it all">
    Click **Original quality…** instead.

    It's quick and keeps everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>
