Ask sits under the player. Type or say what you want, like "goals in the second".
Look closer steps through a moment one frame at a time.

## Ask

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in words">
    Type a question in the Ask box.

    Press `return` to ask.

    <M6_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or say it">
    Hold `T` and say what you want.

    It only listens while you hold.

    <M6_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what was said">
    Click **Listen to this game** when Ask offers it.

    It listens to the game's sound on your Mac.

    <M6_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep what you found">
    Click **Keep as a mark** on a result you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Look closer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Press `L` on a moment.

    <M6_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step through it">
    Press `←` or `→` to move one frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a player">
    Click a player to stay on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark this frame">
    Press `M`.

    Press `Esc` to close.
  </Step>
</Steps>
