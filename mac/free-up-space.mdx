---
title: "How do I free up space on my Mac after a hockey game?"
sidebarTitle: "Free up space"
description: "Click Delete from this Mac on the game, then Move to Trash. Empty the Trash to get the space back."
---

import { M9_1, M9_2 } from "/snippets/video/m-space.jsx";

A game takes a lot of room. When you're done with one, click **Delete from this Mac…** on it.
It goes to the Trash. The space comes back when you empty the Trash.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Delete the game">
    Click **Delete from this Mac…** on the game.

    <M9_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it to the Trash">
    Click **Move to Trash**.

    You can still get it back from the Trash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the space back">
    Empty the Trash when you're sure.

    The space comes back only then.

    <M9_2 />
  </Step>
</Steps>
