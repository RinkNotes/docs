In Lineups, add your team from a screenshot of your team app, and tap **Fill by position**. Drag players to fix the lines.
During the game, tap a player's name and talk. Lineups writes down your note and saves it with that player.

Setup takes about five minutes. Lineups saves each step, so you can stop and come back.

Before you start, take screenshots of the player list in your team app, like TeamSnap.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    In Lineups, tap **Get started**.

    You don't need an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your name">
    Type your name.

    It goes on the lineup cards you share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go on">
    Tap **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name a team">
    Type a team's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the team">
    Tap **+ Add team**.

    Add each team you coach. Each team has its own players, games and notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="All your teams">
    When all your teams are listed, tap **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From screenshots">
    Tap **From screenshots**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the screenshots">
    Pick your screenshots, in order.

    Up to 10. It's fine if they overlap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the list">
    Fix any number, name or position that's wrong.

    Players in amber need a look, most often for a missing number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your roster">
    Tap the button at the bottom, like **Add 18 players**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
No screenshots? Take a photo of a paper list, type the names, or tap **Say it** and say them.
</Tip>

## Your first lineup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who you play">
    Type the opponent's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When you play">
    Pick the date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where you play">
    Tap **Home** or **Away**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the lines">
    Tap **Set the lines**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the card">
    Tap **Fill by position**.

    Goalies go in net, defence in pairs, and forwards in lines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move a player">
    Drag a player to another spot.

    Or tap a player, then tap a spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the game">
    When the card looks right, tap **Start game ›**.

    Not playing yet? Tap **Start later** to save your lines.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first voice note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Close the tip">
    In game mode, tap **Got it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a name">
    Tap a player's name.

    The first time, tap **Allow** for the microphone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say your note">
    Say your note.

    Lineups stops when you pause, and shows what it heard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Save note**.

    Rather type? Tap **Type instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    Tap **Go to my teams**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Lineups turns your voice into text on your phone, and doesn't keep the recording. Your notes are never on a card you share.

<Tip>
Try one voice note while you have Wi-Fi or signal. Your phone may need a one-time download. After that, voice notes work with no signal.
</Tip>
