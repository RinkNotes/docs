Any service that gives you a stream address and a stream key works with RN Video. Paste both under **Another stream address**.
Use an address that starts with rtmps:// if the service has one. It's the safe kind.

## Set up the stream

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    In the Library, tap **Stream**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name both teams">
    Type both teams' names under Teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it goes">
    Tap **Where it goes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick another address">
    Under Other ways, tap **Another stream address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring over the address">
    Paste the address from your service under Stream address.

    An amber line means it starts with rtmp:// and isn't safe. Use rtmps:// if you can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the stream key">
    On your service, copy the stream key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring the key over">
    In RN Video, tap **Paste**.

    It says Saved when the key is in. The key stays on this phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.

    Look for ON AIR at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the period">
    Tap **Start P1** at the faceoff.

    This starts the recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.

    Game over ends the stream too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Using Vimeo? See [stream to Vimeo](/get-started/stream-to-vimeo).
</Tip>
