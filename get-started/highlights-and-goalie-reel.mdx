Your marks become moments. Say what each one was, then tap **Reel ›** and pick **Game reel** or **Goalie reel**.
The app makes the video on your phone. A goalie reel shows each save again, slower, framed on the goalie.

## Tag your moments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    In the Library, tap the game.

    Each letter on the lines is a moment. S is a save, G a goal for, A a goal against, and M a mark with no tag yet.

    <S7_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a moment">
    Tap a moment in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what happened">
    Tap **Save**, **Goal for**, **Goal against** or **Other**.

    <S7_2 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what kind of save">
    For a save, tap the kind of save, like **Glove**.

    <S7_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say whose saves they were">
    Tap **All to Home** if most saves were your goalie's.

    Then fix the few that weren't.

    <S7_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find saves you missed">
    In Suggestions, tap **Suggest moments**.

    It looks through the video on your phone. It shows on newer iPhones and iPads only.

    <S7_14 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep or skip each one">
    Tap **Keep as a save** or **Skip**.

    Watch each one first.

    <S7_15 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make the reel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reel">
    On the game, tap **Reel ›**.

    <S7_16 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a reel">
    Tap **Game reel** or **Goalie reel**.

    A game reel has the score. A goalie reel has no score.

    <S7_17 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tall or wide">
    Tap **9:16** for phones, or **16:9** for a TV.

    <S7_18 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the moments">
    Tap a box to put a moment in or out.

    <S7_19 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take out talking near you">
    In **Reel settings**, pick **Remove nearby voices**.

    Or pick **Off** for no sound.

    <S7_20 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it">
    Tap **Export the game reel**, or **Export the goalie reel**.

    Keep the app open until it's done.

    <S7_21 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Share screen opens when the reel is done. Next, [save it as a video file](/get-started/save-a-video-file).

<Tip>
Want slow motion? Tap a moment and turn on **Slow motion**.
</Tip>

## On a Mac

Send the game to your Mac from the game's **Details**. On the Mac, click **Reel** at the top. Then click **Export**.

See [Pick the reel](/mac/pick-the-reel) and [Export the video](/mac/export).
