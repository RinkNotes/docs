---
title: "How do I record a hockey game on my iPhone?"
sidebarTitle: "Record your first game"
description: "Put your iPhone on a tripod at the glass, tap Game, and mark the big moments as they happen."
---

Put your iPhone on a tripod, aimed at centre ice, and tap **Game**. Tap **MARK** after each big moment, and your reels come from those marks.
You need no signal, no Wi-Fi and no account to record.

## Before the game

Bring a tripod, and power if you can. A game takes about 20 to 25 GB an hour.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Game">
    In RN Video, tap **Game**.

    The first time, a short notice shows. Tap **Start recording** to go on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the rink">
    Type the rink's name under Rink.

    The team names are up to you. They go on your reels and the score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a lens">
    Tap **2x** to start.

    Use **1x** close to the ice. Use **Telephoto** high in the stands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.

    The app keeps these settings for this rink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it use the camera">
    Tap **Allow** for the camera, and again for the microphone.

    It needs both to record the game and its sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aim at the ice">
    Put the phone on the tripod, aimed at centre ice.

    Check that it says Focus locked. If not, aim at the ice and tap **Refocus**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop calls">
    Turn on **Do Not Disturb** in Control Centre.

    A call pauses the recording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the period">
    Tap **Start P1** at the faceoff.

    Want the warm-up too? Tap **Start warm-up** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the big moments">
    Tap **MARK** right after something good happens.

    A volume button marks too. So does a click on an AirPods stem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the saves">
    Tap **Home save** or **Away save** after a save.

    The goalie reel finds them later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the goals">
    Tap **Goal for** when your team scores.

    Tap **Goal against** when the other team scores. The score goes up by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the period">
    Press and hold **Hold to end P1** for one second.

    It's a hold, so a stray tap can't end it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the next period">
    Tap **Start P2** at the faceoff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the score">
    Tap **Yes, the game is over** when the score is right.

    Wrong score? Tap **Fix the score first**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The screen goes dim after 30 seconds to save battery. Tap it to make it bright. **MARK** still works.
</Tip>

## If something goes wrong

- **A call, or you left the app:** it pauses. Open RN Video to keep going.
- **You left at a break:** tap **Continue** under the game in the Library.
- **The phone gets too hot:** let it cool in the shade. Then tap **Resume P1**.
- **The picture is soft:** press and hold **Hold to refocus**.

Every clip is kept.
