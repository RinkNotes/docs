title: "How do I stream a hockey game to a Facebook page or group from my iPhone?"
sidebarTitle: "Stream free to Facebook"
description: "Copy the server and stream key from Facebook's live video, paste them into RN Video, and go live. In a private group, only members watch."
---

In RN Video, tap **Stream** and pick Facebook. Bring over the server and stream key from Facebook's live video, then tap **Go live**.
It's free. In a private group, only the group's members can watch.

## Before you go live

- Ask the rink if streaming is allowed.
- Tell the families the game will be streamed.
- For a group, a group admin gets the key.

## Set up the stream

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    In the Library, tap **Stream**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name both teams">
    Type both teams' names under Teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it goes">
    Tap **Where it goes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Facebook">
    Tap **Facebook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page or group">
    Tap **Page** or **Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the live video on Facebook">
    On Facebook, open Page › Live video › Streaming software, or Group › Live video › Streaming software.

    You can do it on a phone or a computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring over the server">
    Copy the server from Facebook into Server in RN Video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the stream key">
    On Facebook, copy the stream key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring the key over">
    In RN Video, tap **Paste**.

    It says Saved when the key is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go live">
    In RN Video, tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live on Facebook too">
    Press **Go Live** on Facebook once the preview shows there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the period">
    Tap **Start P1** at the faceoff.

    This starts the recording. The score shows on the stream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.

    Game over ends the stream too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

RN Video can't see, post in or manage your page or group. The key is kept on your phone.
