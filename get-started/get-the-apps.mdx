They aren't in the App Store yet. Today you get them free with Apple's TestFlight app.
Each app has a tester guide on rinknotes.app. The guide has the link, and a picture of each screen.

## What you need

| App | Works on | Called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video by RinkNotes | iPhone or iPad with iOS 26, or a Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 | RN Video on your Home Screen |
| Lineups by RinkNotes | iPhone with iOS 17 or later | Lineups on your Home Screen |

## Get an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find TestFlight">
    In the App Store, find TestFlight.

    It's Apple's free app for trying new apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get TestFlight">
    Tap **Get**.

    <S0_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the tester guide">
    On the device you want the app on, open the [Video tester guide](https://rinknotes.app/video/testers/) or the [Lineups tester guide](https://rinknotes.app/lineups/testers/).

    Each guide starts with the TestFlight link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Tap the TestFlight link in the guide.

    If Safari opens, tap **View in TestFlight**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say yes in TestFlight">
    In TestFlight, tap **Accept**.

    <S0_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install it">
    Tap **Install**.

    An orange dot by its name on your Home Screen means it came from TestFlight. That's fine.

    <S0_4 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay up to date">
    In TestFlight, turn on **Automatic Updates**.

    New builds then come by themselves. Each build works for 90 days.

    <S0_5 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Don't delete an app to update it. Your games, teams and notes live only in the app. Deleting it deletes them.
</Warning>
