On your iPhone or iPad, tap **Save to Photos** when the reel is done. On a Mac, click **Export** at the top.
The file is yours. You can send it, post it, or keep it.

## On iPhone or iPad

The Share screen opens when your reel is done.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save it to Photos">
    Tap **Save to Photos**.

    The app only adds to Photos. It never reads them.

    <S7_22 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or share it">
    Tap **Share…**.

    Share… has AirDrop, Messages and Mail too.

    <S7_23 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it to Files">
    Pick **Save to Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every moment as its own clip">
    Tap **Export the highlight pack…**.

    Pick a folder. Families can pick the moments they want.

    <S7_24 />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## On a Mac

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Export">
    Click **Export** at the top of the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a shape">
    Pick **16:9** for a TV or laptop, or **9:16** for phones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the video">
    Click **Export game reel…**.

    The first time, pick a folder and click **Choose**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the file">
    When it's done, click **Show in Finder**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Want the whole game as one video? Under the full game, click **1080p copy…**. It makes a smaller file that's easy to share.
</Tip>
