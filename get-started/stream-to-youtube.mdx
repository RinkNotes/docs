---
title: "How do I stream a hockey game to YouTube from my iPhone?"
sidebarTitle: "Stream free to YouTube"
description: "Get your stream key from YouTube Studio, paste it into RN Video, and tap Go live. It's free, and the game records too."
---

In RN Video, tap **Stream** and pick YouTube. Paste the stream key from YouTube Studio, then tap **Go live**.
It's free. The phone records the game at the same time, and your goals change the score on the stream.

## Before you go live

- Ask the rink if streaming is allowed.
- Tell the families the game will be streamed.
- Use good Wi-Fi or a strong phone signal.
- YouTube can take a day to turn on live, the first time. Do it a day early.

## Set up the stream

Do this before the game, with good internet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    In the Library, tap **Stream**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name both teams">
    Type both teams' names under Teams.

    The score on the stream uses short names, like NOR and HAR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it goes">
    Tap **Where it goes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick YouTube">
    Tap **YouTube**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open YouTube Studio">
    In YouTube Studio, choose **Create**.

    Use a computer or the YouTube app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live on YouTube">
    Choose **Go live**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    Choose **Stream**.

    That's the page with your stream key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Unlisted">
    On YouTube, choose **Unlisted**.

    Only people with the link can watch. Never choose Made for Kids.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the stream key">
    On YouTube, copy the stream key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring the key over">
    In RN Video, tap **Paste**.

    It says Saved when the key is in. The key stays on this phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.

    Look for ON AIR at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the period">
    Tap **Start P1** at the faceoff.

    This starts the recording. **MARK** and the goals work as in a game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the families the link">
    In YouTube, tap Share and send the link.

    RN Video doesn't send it for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the game">
    After the last period, tap **Game over**.

    Game over ends the stream too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the internet drops

The stream says RECONNECTING and tries again by itself. The recording on your phone is fine.

<Tip>
Want to talk over the game? Plug a wired mic into the streaming phone. AirPods don't work for the voice.
</Tip>
