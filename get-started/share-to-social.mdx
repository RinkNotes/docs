Sharing straight to TikTok and Instagram from RN Video is coming. For now, save the reel to Photos.
Then post it from TikTok, Instagram or any app, the way you post any video.

<Info>
Share straight to TikTok and Instagram: Coming.
</Info>

## Today: save it, then post it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a tall reel">
    On the reel, tap **9:16**.

    It's the shape for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. The frame follows the play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take out talking near you">
    In **Reel settings**, pick **Remove nearby voices**.

    Or pick **Off** for no sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it">
    Tap **Export the game reel**, or **Export the goalie reel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it to Photos">
    Tap **Save to Photos**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post it">
    In TikTok, Instagram or another app, pick the video from your photos.

    Post it the way you post any video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Ask the families before you post a video of their kids.
</Warning>
