Vimeo works in RN Video as **Another stream address**. RN Video is free, but you need a Vimeo plan that includes live.
On Vimeo, you copy two things for your live event: the stream address and its key.

<Note>
Vimeo's own words for its buttons can change. If what you see on Vimeo is a bit different, look for its RTMPS settings.
</Note>

## Get the address and key from Vimeo

Do this on a computer, in Vimeo's website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your live event">
    In Vimeo, open your live event, or make a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use an external encoder">
    In the menu next to **Go Live**, pick **Use an external encoder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick RTMPS">
    Under Stream, pick **RTMP/RTMPS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the page open">
    Leave this page open.

    You'll copy the stream address and the key from it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bring them into RN Video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Stream">
    In the Library, tap **Stream**.

    <S3_1 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it goes">
    Tap **Where it goes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick another address">
    Under Other ways, tap **Another stream address**.

    <S3_3 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring over the address">
    Paste Vimeo's stream address under Stream address.

    <S3_6 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the key">
    On Vimeo, copy the stream key.

    Vimeo may call it the stream ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring the key over">
    In RN Video, tap **Paste**.

    It says Saved when the key is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Tap **Ready** at the top right.

    <S3_8 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go live** a few minutes before the game.

    <S3_9 />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live on Vimeo too">
    On Vimeo, click **Go Live** once your stream shows there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rest is just like a game. See [stream to any stream address](/get-started/stream-to-any-address).
